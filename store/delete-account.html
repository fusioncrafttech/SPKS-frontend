How to delete

You can delete your SPKS Exam Academy account in either of these ways:

  • In the app: open Profile → Settings → Delete account, then confirm.
  • On this page: send a deletion request with the email used to register. We will verify the request and delete the account.

What is deleted

We delete your profile (name, email, phone, photo), login credentials, test history, progress, bookmarks, support tickets, and device tokens.

We may keep anonymised or legally required payment invoices for tax and fraud prevention. Active subscriptions are cancelled; refunds follow the published refund policy and Razorpay rules.

Send a deletion request

Use the same email address that is registered in the app. After we receive the request we delete the account within 7 days and email a confirmation.
